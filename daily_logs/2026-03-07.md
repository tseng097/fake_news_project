## 1) New Papers (2–3) and Project Fit

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S., Müller-Budack, E., & Ewerth, R. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks*.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Introduces sentiment-focused adversarial attacks (AdSent framing) and evaluates robustness of fake-news detectors under sentiment manipulations; trains/evaluates with sentiment-aware robustness emphasis.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment is a key, exploitable shortcut signal; adversaries can rewrite sentiment while preserving topical content.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets sentiment vulnerability that many fake-news pipelines under-model; high relevance to `sentiment_invariance`.
- **Limitations:** LLM/attack generation setup can be expensive and may not map 1:1 to low-resource or strict non-LLM pipelines.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **92/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Park, S., Han, S., Xie, X., Lee, J.-G., & Cha, M. (2024). *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection*.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11260
- **Core method:** AdStyle generates diverse style-conversion attack prompts via LLMs and uses augmented adversarial style variants during training.
- **Assumptions:** Writing style is a dominant spurious cue in fake-news detection and can be manipulated.
- **Strengths:** Strong conceptual match for style-robust training (`style_invariance`); emphasizes diversity of attack styles.
- **Limitations:** Dependence on LLM generation quality/cost; requires careful filtering to avoid semantic drift.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **90/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Wang, W.-Y., Chang, Y.-C., & Peng, W.-C. (2024). *Style-News: Incorporating Stylized News Generation and Adversarial Verification for Neural Fake News Detection*.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15509
- **Core method:** Threat-model-based stylized generation conditioned by publisher/template metadata plus adversarial verification using style/source discriminators.
- **Assumptions:** Publisher/style priors are exploitable and can be modeled explicitly in both attack and defense.
- **Strengths:** Useful framing for separating content truth cues from style/source cues; informs robust evaluation design.
- **Limitations:** Metadata dependence and architecture complexity are heavier than current project scope.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

## 2) Synthesis vs Current fake_news_project Methods
- Current project already has the right robustness axes (`lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`) aligned with these papers.
- Gap observed: lexical synonym perturbation can accidentally alter sentiment-bearing tokens, causing objective leakage between lexical and sentiment robustness tracks.
- Paper A/B together suggest cleaner factorization of perturbation types improves robustness attribution.
- Therefore today’s change isolates lexical mHC-lite perturbations from sentiment pivots to reduce label/semantics drift and keep strategy boundaries clean.

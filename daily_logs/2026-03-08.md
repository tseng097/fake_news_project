## 1) New Papers (topic-locked)

> Note: `web_search` (Brave API) was unavailable in this environment. Papers were sourced via arXiv query + direct arXiv fetch.

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S., Müller-Budack, E., & Ewerth, R. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks*. arXiv:2601.15277.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Controlled sentiment-based adversarial rewriting + sentiment-agnostic robustness training (AdSent framing) to maintain veracity consistency under sentiment shifts.
- **Assumptions:** Fake-news detectors rely on sentiment shortcuts and can be attacked by polarity/style rewrites that preserve core claims.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets sentiment vulnerability; includes robustness/generalization evaluation framing.
- **Limitations:** LLM-based adversarial generation can be costly and potentially distribution-specific.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **94/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Park, S., Han, S., Xie, X., Lee, J.-G., & Cha, M. (2024/2025). *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection*. arXiv:2406.11260.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11260
- **Core method:** AdStyle uses LLM-driven prompt diversification to generate style-converted adversarial views for robustness training.
- **Assumptions:** Style cues are exploitable shortcuts in fake-news detection and adversaries can rewrite style while keeping semantics.
- **Strengths:** Strong style-attack coverage and practical augmentation recipe for invariance training.
- **Limitations:** Depends on quality/cost of LLM generations and filtering quality.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **91/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Wang, W.-Y., Chang, Y.-C., & Peng, W.-C. (2024). *Incorporating Stylized News Generation and Adversarial Verification for Neural Fake News Detection (Style-News)*. arXiv:2401.15509.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15509
- **Core method:** Threat-model setup with style-aware neural generation and adversarial verification via style/source discriminators.
- **Assumptions:** Publisher/style priors can be manipulated and should be explicitly defended against.
- **Strengths:** Clear attack-defense co-design; useful perspective on disentangling source/style from truth cues.
- **Limitations:** Heavier metadata/modeling requirements than this project’s lightweight pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **85/100**

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Wu, J., Guo, J., & Hooi, B. (2023). *Fake News in Sheep's Clothing: Robust Fake News Detection Against LLM-Empowered Style Attacks*. arXiv:2310.10830.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10830
- **Core method:** Defines an LLM-powered style-camouflage attack setting and introduces robustness-oriented training/evaluation against style mimicry of trustworthy publishers.
- **Assumptions:** Detectors overfit to style cues; adversaries can preserve claim content while changing writing style.
- **Strengths:** Clear and practical threat model; quantifies large F1 drops under style attacks; directly actionable for style invariance work.
- **Limitations:** LLM attack distributions may differ from in-the-wild paraphrase/style edits.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **95/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Wu, J., Guo, J., & Hooi, B. (2023). *Adapting Fake News Detection to the Era of Large Language Models*. arXiv:2311.04917.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04917
- **Core method:** Studies detector adaptation when both real and fake news can be machine-generated/paraphrased; highlights robustness gaps under machine paraphrase shifts.
- **Assumptions:** Future fake-news corpora are mixed human/LLM authored and detector brittleness is amplified by paraphrastic shifts.
- **Strengths:** Strong motivation for lexical/style robustness beyond static dataset assumptions.
- **Limitations:** Broad adaptation framing; less focused on lightweight training objectives than this repo.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** He, J., Zhang, H., Xiao, Y., Guo, W., Yao, S., & Liu, R. (2025). *FactGuard: Event-Centric and Commonsense-Guided Fake News Detection*. arXiv:2511.10281.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10281
- **Core method:** Moves detection from style-heavy cues toward event/content and commonsense signals, reducing vulnerability to style imitation attacks.
- **Assumptions:** Style-only signals are brittle under adversarial rewriting; event-centric evidence is more robust.
- **Strengths:** Useful blueprint for reducing shortcut reliance and improving robustness under style manipulation.
- **Limitations:** Heavier architecture/inference than current lightweight RoBERTa pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

   - verifies symmetric KL definition,

### Why it is meaningful
- Paper-grounded by style/paraphrase attack findings: when perturbations are intended as meaning-preserving, bidirectional agreement reduces clean-to-aug direction bias.
- Keeps scope lock intact: no new strategy introduced, only mHC-lite objective quality improved.

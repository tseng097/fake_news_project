### Paper A
- **Citation:** Liu, Y.-H., et al. (2026). *Retrieval-Augmented Detector with Adversarial Refinement for Robust Fake News Detection (RADAR)*. arXiv:2601.03981.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03981
- **Core method:** Joint generator-detector setup where a generator produces fact-perturbed rewrites and the detector uses retrieval-backed verification; co-evolution is driven by verbal adversarial feedback (natural-language critiques instead of scalar rewards).
- **Assumptions:** Realistic fake-news attacks involve fluent rewriting and factual perturbation; retrieval evidence helps resist style-only or fluency-based deception.
- **Strengths:** Explicit adversarial refinement loop; practical robustness framing; retrieval adds grounding against paraphrase/style laundering.
- **Limitations:** More complex than this repo’s lightweight text-only training setup; requires retrieval infra and stronger compute.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **89/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Das, R. K., et al. (2025). *Fake News Detection After LLM Laundering: Measurement and Explanation*. arXiv:2501.18649.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.18649
- **Core method:** Evaluates detector performance under LLM paraphrase laundering and uses explanation analysis (LIME) to diagnose failures; reports sentiment-shift effects despite high semantic-overlap metrics.
- **Assumptions:** Adversaries can preserve topical semantics while changing lexical/style cues via paraphrase models.
- **Strengths:** Directly aligned with lexical/style robustness and perturbation quality control; identifies concrete failure mode (sentiment drift).
- **Limitations:** Evaluation-heavy; does not provide a single low-cost training recipe by itself.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **94/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Gong, C., et al. (2025/2026). *Group-Adaptive Adversarial Learning for Robust Fake News Detection Against Malicious Comments (AdComment)*. arXiv:2510.09712.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09712
- **Core method:** Categorizes adversarial comment types (fact distortion, logical confusion, emotional manipulation) and adaptively reweights adversarial exposure during training (IDR mechanism).
- **Assumptions:** Attack distributions are heterogeneous; fixed adversarial mixture under-trains hardest vulnerabilities.
- **Strengths:** Actionable concept for adaptive robustness budgeting; emotional manipulation category overlaps sentiment robustness concerns.
- **Limitations:** Focuses on comment-augmented setups rather than article-only text pipelines; includes LLM synthesis dependencies.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **81/100**

## 1) New Papers (topic-locked)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Park, S., Han, S., Xie, X., Lee, J.-G., & Cha, M. (2024/2025). *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection (AdStyle).* arXiv:2406.11260.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11260
- **Core method:** Uses LLMs to generate diverse style-conversion attack prompts and augments training with these hard style variants to improve detector robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Style cues are exploitable shortcuts; increasing style diversity during training improves invariance.
- **Strengths:** Strongly aligned with style-attack robustness; directly actionable for style invariance training/evaluation.
- **Limitations:** Depends on LLM-generated style transformations; heavier than this repo’s lightweight local augmentation path.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **92/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S., Müller-Budack, E., & Ewerth, R. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks (AdSent).* arXiv:2601.15277.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Constructs controlled sentiment-based adversarial attacks and trains detectors for sentiment-agnostic consistency between original and sentiment-shifted variants.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment is a vulnerable shortcut for fake-news classifiers; controlled sentiment shifts can expose and reduce this bias.
- **Strengths:** Direct one-to-one relevance to this repo’s `sentiment_invariance` branch.
- **Limitations:** LLM-based attack generation may be expensive; attack realism depends on prompt quality.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **96/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Liu, Q., Wu, J., Wu, S., & Wang, L. (2024). *Out-of-distribution Evidence-aware Fake News Detection via Dual Adversarial Debiasing (DAL).* arXiv:2304.12888.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12888
- **Core method:** Adds dual adversarial discriminators (news-side and evidence-side) to suppress spurious content-label biases and improve OOD robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Performance drops are driven by shortcut correlations and dataset bias; adversarial debiasing can improve generalization.
- **Strengths:** Robustness framing and adversarial debiasing logic are useful for understanding shortcut mitigation.
- **Limitations:** Evidence-aware setup differs from this project’s text-only LIAR pipeline; less direct for lexical/style/sentiment perturbation mechanics.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **78/100**

## 2) Synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods
- Current strategy scope already covers the most actionable robustness axes from these papers: lexical/synonym perturbation, style invariance, and sentiment invariance.
- AdStyle reinforces the need for **diverse** style attacks (not one deterministic rewrite pattern).
- AdSent supports controlled sentiment attacks with consistent veracity targets; this directly motivates stronger perturbation diversity and reproducibility controls in `sentiment_shift_simple`.
- DAL is conceptually useful for shortcut debiasing, but its evidence-aware architecture is out of current implementation scope.

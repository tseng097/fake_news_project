## 1) New papers (topic-locked)

> Web note: integrated metasearch path was unavailable in this runtime, so discovery used direct arXiv query/fetch.

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Zellers, R., Holtzman, A., Rashkin, H., Bisk, Y., Farhadi, A., Roesner, F., & Choi, Y. (2019/2020). *Defending Against Neural Fake News.* arXiv:1905.12616.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.12616
- **Core method:** Threat-models neural fake-news generation (Grover) and evaluates detector robustness; shows generator-aware detection can significantly improve fake-vs-real discrimination.
- **Assumptions:** Neural generators leave detectable artifacts; adversarially realistic machine-written news is a core robustness threat.
- **Strengths:** Foundational adversarial fake-news defense framing; strong threat-model perspective for robustness evaluation design.
- **Limitations:** Earlier-generation model ecosystem; less focused on modern lexical/style/sentiment disentanglement.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **86/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Zhuo, T. Y., et al. (2023). *Fake News Detectors are Biased against Texts Generated by Large Language Models.* arXiv:2309.08674.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08674
- **Core method:** Demonstrates detector bias patterns between human-written vs LLM-generated content; proposes adversarial training with LLM-paraphrased genuine news to mitigate bias.
- **Assumptions:** Linguistic signatures of LLM text can spuriously drive fake-news predictions.
- **Strengths:** Directly relevant to style/lexical robustness and distribution-shift concerns; includes practical mitigation direction.
- **Limitations:** Requires careful dataset construction and paraphrase quality control; mitigation may partially overfit specific generation styles.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **91/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Kelk, I., Finkelstein, A., Solaiman, I., Dowie, J., & Slonim, N. (2022). *Automatic Fake News Detection: Are current models “fact-checking” or “gut-checking”?* arXiv:2204.07229.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.07229
- **Core method:** Tests whether fake-news models rely on manipulable stylistic/emotional cues; applies pre-processing/style-transfer flattening and shows dependence on non-factual cues.
- **Assumptions:** Emotion/sentiment/style artifacts are exploitable shortcuts in current detectors.
- **Strengths:** Strong conceptual fit for style/sentiment invariance objectives and augmentation design.
- **Limitations:** Primarily diagnostic; does not provide a single lightweight end-to-end training recipe.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **93/100**

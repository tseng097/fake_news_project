## 1) New papers (topic-locked)

> Web note: local metasearch path was unavailable in this runtime, so discovery used direct arXiv query/fetch.

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S., Müller-Budack, E., & Ewerth, R. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks.* arXiv:2601.15277.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Introduces **AdSent**, generating controlled sentiment-shift adversarial variants and training sentiment-agnostic detectors to keep veracity predictions stable under sentiment manipulation.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment is a shortcut feature and can be manipulated without changing factuality.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets sentiment attack robustness; evaluates cross-dataset/adversarial generalization.
- **Limitations:** Relies on quality of sentiment perturbation generation; LLM-generated attacks may not cover all real-world sentiment manipulations.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **95/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Park, S., Han, S., Xie, X., Lee, J.-G., & Cha, M. (2024/2025). *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection.* arXiv:2406.11260.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11260
- **Core method:** Proposes **AdStyle**, using LLMs to generate diverse style-conversion attacks and augment training for style-robust fake news detection.
- **Assumptions:** Style conversion can preserve semantics while flipping stylistic cues detectors over-rely on.
- **Strengths:** Strong fit with style invariance goals; practical augmentation framework.
- **Limitations:** LLM prompt design quality strongly affects perturbation quality/diversity; compute overhead can be high.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **93/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Tong, Z., Gong, C., Gu, Y., Shi, H., Liu, Q., Wu, S., & Zhang, X.-Y. (2025/2026). *Group-Adaptive Adversarial Learning for Robust Fake News Detection Against Malicious Comments.* arXiv:2510.09712.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09712
- **Core method:** Introduces **AdComment** with grouped adversarial comment types (fact distortion, logical confusion, emotional manipulation) and adaptive resampling (IDR) to focus on vulnerability regions.
- **Assumptions:** Attack diversity can be grouped meaningfully; adaptive sampling improves worst-case robustness.
- **Strengths:** Explicit adversarial curriculum and adaptive balancing; robust optimization perspective.
- **Limitations:** Comment-augmented setting is not identical to pure content-only fake-news classification; additional complexity from group scheduling.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **87/100**

## 1) New papers (topic-locked)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Wu, J., Guo, J., & Hooi, B. (2024). *Fake News in Sheep's Clothing: Robust Fake News Detection Against LLM-Empowered Style Attacks.* KDD 2024. arXiv:2310.10830.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10830
- **Core method:** Proposes **SheepDog** with style-diverse LLM reframings + style-agnostic consistency training so detectors focus on content rather than writing style.
- **Assumptions:** Style features are easy adversarial targets and should be treated as non-causal shortcuts for veracity.
- **Strengths:** Very direct fit for `style_invariance`; strong empirical framing of style-camouflage attacks.
- **Limitations:** Depends on LLM reframing pipeline and prompt quality; heavier than this repo’s lightweight local augmentation.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **95/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Su, J., Cardie, C., & Nakov, P. (2024). *Adapting Fake News Detection to the Era of Large Language Models.* Findings of NAACL 2024. arXiv:2311.04917.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04917
- **Core method:** Studies robustness under human-written vs machine-generated/paraphrased real/fake news mixtures; analyzes detector bias and provides practical training-ratio guidance.
- **Assumptions:** Distribution shift from LLM rewriting/paraphrasing is a central robustness challenge for modern fake-news detection.
- **Strengths:** Strong relevance to lexical/style perturbation realism and train-test shift analysis.
- **Limitations:** Primarily evaluation/analysis guidance rather than a drop-in lightweight augmentation algorithm.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Haqiqi, M., Younis, M. M., Chen, C.-M., et al. (2026). *JailNewsBench: Multi-Lingual and Regional Benchmark for Fake News Generation under Jailbreak Attacks.* arXiv:2603.01291.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01291
- **Core method:** Introduces a benchmark for jailbreak-induced fake-news generation across languages/regions to stress-test downstream defenses.
- **Assumptions:** Attack surfaces differ substantially across language/region; robustness evaluation must include jailbreak-enabled generation threats.
- **Strengths:** Useful threat-model expansion for future robustness evaluation suites.
- **Limitations:** Benchmark-generation focused; less directly actionable for the current local text-augmentation training loop.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **76/100**

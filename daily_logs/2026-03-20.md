### Paper A
- **Citation:** Bethany, M., Bortsova, G., Oren, Y., Last, M., Taieb-Maimon, M., Jitsev, J., Chechik, G., & Schwartz, R. (2025). **CAMOUFLAGE: Exploiting Misinformation Detection Systems Through LLM-driven Adversarial Claim Transformation.** arXiv:2505.01900.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01900
- **Core method:** Iterative two-agent LLM attack pipeline (attacker + prompt optimizer) that creates semantically equivalent claim rewrites to break retrieval and claim-evidence comparison in evidence-based misinformation detectors.
- **Assumptions:** Adversaries can perform higher-level lexical/structural/style rewrites while preserving claim meaning; detector access may be black-box binary outputs only.
- **Strengths:** Directly relevant to robustness under meaning-preserving rewrites; goes beyond token substitutions and captures realistic adaptive attack behavior.
- **Limitations:** Attack-focused; does not directly provide a defensive training recipe for standard sequence-classification fake-news pipelines.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **94/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Zhu, Y., Sheng, Q., Cao, J., Li, S., Wang, D., & Li, J. (2022). **MDFEND: Multi-domain Fake News Detection.** arXiv:2201.00987 (CIKM 2021).
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.00987
- **Core method:** Mixture-of-experts fake-news detector with domain gate to handle domain shift across topic/source domains.
- **Assumptions:** Domain/style distribution shift is a central failure mode; explicitly modeling domain variability improves generalization.
- **Strengths:** Strong motivation for style/domain invariance and reducing reliance on superficial source-style features.
- **Limitations:** Not explicitly adversarial; architecture-centric and broader than this repository’s constrained strategy set.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Schuster, T., Shah, R., Gupta, R., Lewis, M., Yuret, D., & Weston, J. (2020). **The Limitations of Stylometry for Detecting Machine-Generated Fake News.** arXiv:1908.09805.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.09805
- **Core method:** Benchmark analysis showing stylometric cues can separate provenance but fail to separate malicious vs benign machine-generated content under stylistic similarity.
- **Assumptions:** Stylistic markers are unstable/non-causal for veracity; robust detection must avoid over-reliance on style artifacts.
- **Strengths:** Clean warning against style-shortcut dependence; directly supports invariance-style preprocessing choices.
- **Limitations:** Focuses on machine-generated misinformation setting; older LM ecosystem.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **86/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods

- Current strategy lock remains unchanged: `vanilla`, `lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`.
- CAMOUFLAGE indicates that **style/structure-level rewrites** can be more effective than token-level edits, reinforcing the need for robust style normalization in `style_invariance`.
- MDFEND reinforces that **domain/style artifacts** are shortcut features; reducing platform/source wrappers (URLs, @mentions) should improve invariance.
- Stylometry-limitations work reinforces that detector success from style fingerprints may not transfer to true veracity robustness.
- Net takeaway: strengthen **artifact-stripping style perturbations** while keeping mHC-lite lexical consistency path unchanged.

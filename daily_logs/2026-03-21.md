### Paper A
- **Citation:** Tian, C., Ho, Q., & Chen, X. (2025). **A Symbolic Adversarial Learning Framework for Evolving Fake News Generation and Detection (SALF).** arXiv:2508.19633.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19633
- **Core method:** Two-agent symbolic adversarial loop: a generation agent and detection agent iteratively co-train through prompt-level “symbolic” updates rather than gradient updates, producing progressively stronger attacks and defenses.
- **Assumptions:** Prompt-level optimization can approximate adversarial learning dynamics; iterative co-evolution better reflects real attacker adaptation.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets evolving adversarial fake-news text; quantifies large degradation under stronger attacks and recovery after adversarial refinement.
- **Limitations:** Framework is heavy for this repo’s lightweight training loop; prompt-agent setup differs from current RoBERTa fine-tuning regime.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **90/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Essahli, S., Sarsar, O., Bentajer, A., Motii, A., & Fouad, I. (2025). **FakeZero: Real-Time, Privacy-Preserving Misinformation Detection for Facebook and X.** arXiv:2510.25932.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25932
- **Core method:** Practical pipeline for social-platform fake-news detection with domain-adaptive fine-tuning, adversarial augmentation, and compact deployment constraints.
- **Assumptions:** Platform wrappers/artifacts and domain shift are major robustness stressors in in-the-wild text streams.
- **Strengths:** Highly deployment-grounded; emphasizes robustness under social-media text noise and wrappers.
- **Limitations:** Includes engineering/deployment constraints beyond this repo; adversarial augmentation details are not focused only on lexical/style/sentiment invariance.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Hoy, N., & Koulouri, T. (2025). **An exploration of features to improve the generalisability of fake news detection models.** arXiv:2502.20299.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20299
- **Core method:** Analyzes why token-heavy models fail cross-dataset generalization and evaluates stylistic + social/monetization feature families as more robust alternatives.
- **Assumptions:** Coarse labels induce source/publisher bias; style/social artifacts can be either useful robust cues or confounds depending on handling.
- **Strengths:** Strong generalization framing, explicitly tied to style/lexical sensitivity and dataset-bias mitigation.
- **Limitations:** Feature-engineering centric and not a direct adversarial training recipe; dataset setting differs from LIAR.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods

- Strategy lock remains unchanged: `vanilla`, `lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`.
- SALF reinforces that attacks evolve beyond static substitutions; our robustness path should keep perturbations diverse but controlled.
- FakeZero + Hoy/Koulouri both reinforce that **platform-style wrappers** (social formatting artifacts) can become shortcut cues and harm transfer robustness.
- For this repo, the best low-risk action is to strengthen `style_invariance` normalization of social wrappers while keeping lexical/sentiment strategy boundaries intact.

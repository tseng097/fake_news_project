### Paper A
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S., Müller-Budack, E., & Ewerth, R. (2026). **Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks.** arXiv:2601.15277.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Introduces an adversarial sentiment attack/evaluation setup (AdSent-style) to test whether fake-news detectors remain stable when sentiment is strategically manipulated while topical content is preserved.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment cues are a major exploitable shortcut; attackers can rewrite tone/emotion faster than they can change factual content.
- **Strengths:** Directly aligned with our `sentiment_invariance` strategy; highlights realistic attack surface for modern LLM-assisted misinformation.
- **Limitations:** Focuses heavily on LLM-based detector/attacker combinations and may not transfer 1:1 to compact RoBERTa pipelines.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **94/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Ma, S.-D., Liu, Y.-H., Lin, H.-Y., Chen, P.-Y., Huang, H.-Y., & Hsu, S.-Y. (2026). **RADAR: Retrieval-Augmented Detector with Adversarial Refinement for Robust Fake News Detection.** arXiv:2601.03981.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03981
- **Core method:** Detector-generator co-evolution where fake-news rewrites are iteratively hardened using verbal adversarial feedback; detector uses retrieval-backed verification signals.
- **Assumptions:** Attackers adapt over time; static perturbation pools under-estimate true robustness requirements.
- **Strengths:** Strong robustness framing and iterative adversarial refinement concept useful for designing stronger augmentation schedules.
- **Limitations:** Retrieval stack + co-evolution machinery is broader/heavier than this repo’s current training loop and strategy lock.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **87/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Zhang, M., Liu, Q., Tao, X., Wu, S., & Wang, L. (2025). **SINCon: Mitigate LLM-Generated Malicious Message Injection Attack for Rumor Detection.** arXiv:2504.07135.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.07135
- **Core method:** Defends rumor detectors against injected adversarial messages by reducing concentration on single high-influence nodes via contrastive objectives.
- **Assumptions:** Attackers exploit brittle influence concentration and can target model-sensitive spans/messages.
- **Strengths:** Reinforces need for robustness against adaptive text injections and shortcut exploitation.
- **Limitations:** Tree-structured rumor setup differs from single-article fake-news classification; indirect transfer to this repo.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **78/100**

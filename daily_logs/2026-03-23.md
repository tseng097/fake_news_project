# Fake News Robustness Daily Log — 2026-03-23

## 1) New Papers (topic-locked: adversarial NLP robustness for fake news)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S., et al. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks*. arXiv:2601.15277.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Introduces **AdSent**: sentiment-targeted adversarial rewriting + sentiment-agnostic training enforcing consistent veracity predictions between original and sentiment-shifted variants.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment shifts should not flip factual veracity label; LLM-generated sentiment edits are realistic threat model.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets sentiment vulnerability; evaluates both clean performance and robustness under attack.
- **Limitations:** Relies on generation quality/cost of LLM-based attacks; may inherit attack distribution bias.
- **Worth-Reading (for this repo):** **93/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Park, S., et al. (2025). *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection*. arXiv:2406.11260 (WWW'25).
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11260
- **Core method:** **AdStyle** style-conversion adversarial augmentation using diverse prompt templates generated/curated via LLMs.
- **Assumptions:** Many detectors overfit to stylistic artifacts; style-preserving content edits should keep veracity labels.
- **Strengths:** Explicitly style-focused robustness objective; strong fit with style_invariance line.
- **Limitations:** LLM prompt/search process may be heavy; transferability to non-LLM perturbations varies.
- **Worth-Reading (for this repo):** **90/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Wang, W.-Y., et al. (2024). *Incorporating Stylized News Generation and Adversarial Verification for Neural Fake News Detection (Style-News)*. arXiv:2401.15509 (EACL 2024).
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15509
- **Core method:** Trains style-aware generator + discriminators (style/source) for adversarial verification under publisher-style mimicry.
- **Assumptions:** Publisher style and source cues are exploitable attack surfaces for neural fake news.
- **Strengths:** Concrete adversarial verification framing; includes style adherence/content preservation metrics.
- **Limitations:** Depends on publisher metadata availability; more complex than lightweight baseline pipelines.
- **Worth-Reading (for this repo):** **84/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current project methods
- Current repo already has strategy lock (`vanilla`, `lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`) and simple perturbation pipelines.
- Relative to AdSent/AdStyle/Style-News, the major gap is **instance-level reliability control**: not all augmented pairs are equally trustworthy.
- Practical, scope-safe takeaway: keep current strategies, but reduce noisy consistency pressure for low-confidence examples.

## 3) Paper-grounded enhancement implemented today
- Added **confidence-gated consistency weighting** for style/sentiment invariance losses.
- New config field: `StrategyConfig.confidence_threshold` (default `0.0` => backward-compatible behavior).
- New CLI/train config arg: `--consistency-conf-threshold`.
- Effect: consistency penalty is scaled by fraction of clean predictions whose max-prob >= threshold.
- Rationale: aligns with adversarial sentiment/style findings where some perturbations are ambiguous; avoids over-regularizing uncertain samples.

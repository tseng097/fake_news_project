# Fake News Robustness Daily Log — 2026-03-25

## 1) New papers (topic-locked)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Wang, C., et al. (2026). *Prompt-Induced Linguistic Fingerprints for LLM-Generated Fake News Detection (LIFE).* WWW 2026 / arXiv:2508.12632v2.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12632
- **Core method:** Detects fake-news generation artifacts through prompt-induced token-probability distribution shifts; reconstructs word-level probability fingerprints and highlights key fragments for classification.
- **Assumptions:** Malicious prompting leaves stable linguistic/probabilistic traces even when surface text is fluent.
- **Strengths:** Strong fit for robustness against paraphrastic laundering; focuses on subtle distributional cues instead of shallow keywords.
- **Limitations:** Primarily targets LLM-generated fake news; transfer to purely human-authored misinformation may vary.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** *Evaluating the robustness of fake news detectors to adversarial attacks with real user comments.* International Journal of Data Science and Analytics (2025). DOI: 10.1007/s41060-025-00790-3.
- **Link:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41060-025-00790-3
- **Core method:** Introduces attacks that use real, pre-existing human comments (topic-specific and generic retrieval) rather than synthetic comments, then measures detector degradation.
- **Assumptions:** Natural human-written perturbations are harder to detect and can be stronger robustness tests than synthetic generations.
- **Strengths:** High ecological validity; highlights robustness gaps under realistic social-media text distributions.
- **Limitations:** Comment-augmented setting is not identical to this repo’s single-text pipeline; adaptation needed.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **82/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** *Hybrid optimization driven fake news detection using reinforced transformer models.* Scientific Reports (2025). DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-99936-3.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-99936-3
- **Core method:** Transformer pipeline with reinforced/hybrid optimization aimed at stronger generalization under topic/domain shift.
- **Assumptions:** Better optimization + representation improves robustness to distribution shifts in fake news.
- **Strengths:** Practical robustness orientation at system level.
- **Limitations:** Less targeted to lexical/style/sentiment adversarial perturbations specifically.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **69/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods
- Current project already has the right strategy lock for this thread (`vanilla`, `lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`).

## 3) Small paper-grounded enhancement implemented
- **Enhanced `lexical_mhc_lite` synonym filtering** in `src/augment.py`:
  - Added `_max_wordnet_path_similarity(source, replacement)`.
  - Added a semantic closeness gate (`>= 0.2`) for candidate synonym replacements.
  - Changed candidate selection to prefer top semantic candidates (top-3, still random with seed).
- Why this is meaningful:
  - Keeps lexical perturbations closer to paraphrastic edits, reducing semantic drift in consistency training.
  - Preserves attack diversity without adding new strategies or leaving project scope.

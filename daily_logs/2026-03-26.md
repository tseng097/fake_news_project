# Daily Fake-News Robustness Cycle — 2026-03-26

## 1) New papers (strictly in fake-news robustness / adversarial NLP)

### Paper A — Style robustness
- **Citation:** Park, S. et al. (2024/2025). *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection*. arXiv:2406.11260 (WWW'25 track).
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11260
- **Core method:** AdStyle uses LLM-generated diverse style-conversion attack prompts for adversarial augmentation, training detectors to preserve veracity under stylistic rewrites.
- **Assumptions:** Style edits keep core factual meaning mostly intact; generated style attacks are representative of deployment-time manipulations.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets style-based evasion; diversity of attacks is explicit; reports gains on robustness and base detection.
- **Limitations:** LLM-generated attacks may be distribution-dependent; may still miss lexical/semantic edge cases beyond style.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **91/100**

### Paper B — Sentiment robustness
- **Citation:** Tahmasebi, S. et al. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks*. arXiv:2601.15277.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** AdSent framework: controlled sentiment adversarial rewriting + sentiment-agnostic consistency training so veracity predictions stay stable across sentiment shifts.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment perturbations should not change truthfulness labels; detector bias toward sentiment exists and is exploitable.
- **Strengths:** Very close fit to our `sentiment_invariance` strategy; highlights realistic vulnerability with measurable F1 degradation and recovery.
- **Limitations:** LLM attack pipeline cost/variance; sentiment control quality may vary by domain/language.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **95/100**

### Paper C — Publisher/style-aware adversarial verification
- **Citation:** Tsai, C.-T. et al. (2024). *Style-News: Incorporating Stylized News Generation and Adversarial Verification for Neural Fake News Detection*. EACL 2024.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.eacl-long.92/
- **Core method:** Trains style-aware generator + discriminators (style/source) to stress-test and improve fake-news/AI-generated-news verification, using publisher-style conditioning.
- **Assumptions:** Publisher/style metadata captures meaningful stylistic priors; style-source discrimination improves robustness.
- **Strengths:** Explicit threat-model framing; adversarial generate-and-verify loop aligned with robustness goals.
- **Limitations:** Metadata dependence; neural-fake-news emphasis may not transfer fully to human-written misinformation.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

1. For `lexical_mhc_lite`, should we formally treat discourse connectives as a protected class (like sentiment pivots) across all datasets, or make it dataset-adaptive?

4. Should consistency regularization be weighted by estimated semantic-preservation confidence from augmentation itself (not only model confidence)?
5. Is there value in a small held-out “framing stress set” (connector swaps, hedges, emphasis shifts) as a recurring robustness gate?

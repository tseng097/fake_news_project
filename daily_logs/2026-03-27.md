## 1) New papers (topic-locked: fake-news robustness + adversarial NLP)

### Paper A — Semantic robustness under lexical/style perturbations
- **Citation:** Wu, Y., Xiao, Y., Hu, M., Liu, M., Wang, P., & Liu, M. (2024). *Towards Robust Evidence-Aware Fake News Detection via Improving Semantic Perception*. LREC-COLING 2024, pp. 16607–16618.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.1443/
- **Core method:** Model-agnostic robust training with two augmentations: (i) semantic-flipped claims (same surface cues, opposite meaning), and (ii) semantic-invariant style rewrites (same meaning, different expression), plus a semantics-sensitive representation module and multi-objective optimization.
- **Assumptions:** Synthetic semantic flips/styles are realistic enough proxies for adversarial manipulations; robustness improves when semantics and style are disentangled.
- **Strengths:** Very aligned with our lexical/style invariance goals; explicitly stress-tests semantic sensitivity (modifier/compositional brittleness).
- **Limitations:** Evidence-aware setup may not transfer 1:1 to plain text-only pipeline; synthetic transformations can still miss real-world attack diversity.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **92/100**

### Paper B — Domain/category style robustness via contrastive learning
- **Citation:** Wu, D., Tan, Z., Zhao, H., Jiang, T., & Geng, N. (2024). *Domain- and category-style clustering for general fake news detection via contrastive learning*. Information Processing & Management, 61(4), 103725.
- **Link:** https://dblp.org/rec/journals/ipm/WuTZJG24
- **Core method:** Contrastive framework that organizes style signals at domain/category levels to improve generalization to cross-event/cross-domain fake-news settings.
- **Assumptions:** Style clusters can be learned in a way that helps transfer rather than overfitting source-specific artifacts.
- **Strengths:** Directly relevant to `style_invariance`; offers concrete contrastive perspective for making representations less brittle to source/style shift.
- **Limitations:** More complex than our current lightweight pipeline; publication page/abstract access was limited in this environment, so details are taken from bibliographic and search metadata.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper C — Adversarial training baseline for robust fake-news classification
- **Citation:** *ANN: adversarial news net for robust fake news classification* (Scientific Reports, 2024), DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-56567-4.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-56567-4
- **Core method:** Trains detectors on clean + perturbed examples (FGSM-style perturbation) and augments sentiment lexicon handling (e.g., emoticons/slang) to improve robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Gradient-based perturbations and noisy social-language features approximate deployment attacks and improve robustness through exposure.
- **Strengths:** Practical adversarial-training signal; supports sentiment/style noise handling under realistic social-media writing artifacts.
- **Limitations:** Perturbation realism can be weaker than semantically controlled attacks; method is broad and less targeted than explicit lexical/style/sentiment invariance objectives.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **80/100**

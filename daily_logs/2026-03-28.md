## 1) New papers (topic-locked: fake-news robustness + adversarial NLP)

### Paper A — Sentiment adversarial robustness in fake-news detection
- **Citation:** Hu, Y. et al. (2026). *Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks*.
- **Link:** http://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277
- **Core method:** Introduces adversarial sentiment perturbations for fake-news text and proposes an AdSent-style training/evaluation setup to keep veracity predictions stable when sentiment wording is manipulated.
- **Assumptions:** Sentiment manipulation is a realistic attack vector; preserving label-consistent predictions under sentiment shifts improves real-world robustness.
- **Strengths:** Direct fit to our `sentiment_invariance` line; explicit threat model for sentiment-based evasion.
- **Limitations:** arXiv preprint (not yet archival venue); implementation details may still evolve.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **93/100**

### Paper B — Linguistic/stylometric detection for deceptive news-like content
- **Citation:** Gehrke, M., et al. (2024). *Do You Speak Disinformation? Computational Detection of Deceptive News-Like Content Using Linguistic and Stylistic Features*. Digital Journalism.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1080/21670811.2024.2305792
- **Core method:** Feature-based and explainable ML analysis of deceptive news-like text with strong emphasis on linguistic + style signals and their contribution to prediction.
- **Assumptions:** Stylistic and lexical features contain consistent deception cues that can be exploited by models.
- **Strengths:** Strongly relevant for identifying style-based shortcut risks and what to normalize in `style_invariance`.
- **Limitations:** Feature-engineered framing may not transfer directly to deep end-to-end pipelines without adaptation.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper C — Detection limits via linguistic traits in AI mis/disinformation
- **Citation:** Yang, X. et al. (2025). *Linguistic features of AI mis/disinformation and the detection limits of LLMs*. Nature Communications.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-67145-1
- **Core method:** Builds multilingual mis/disinformation datasets and analyzes psycholinguistic/computational linguistic factors (including sentiment and syntax) affecting detectability.
- **Assumptions:** Linguistic feature shifts (sentiment/cognition/personal concerns/syntax) are major determinants of detector failure.
- **Strengths:** High-quality empirical evidence that non-semantic language style can drive both success and failure in detection.
- **Limitations:** Focuses on AI-generated mis/disinformation and includes Chinese-heavy analysis; transfer to classic fake-news corpora should be validated.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **85/100**

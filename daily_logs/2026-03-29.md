## 1) New papers (topic-locked: fake-news robustness + adversarial NLP)

### Paper A — LM-generated adversarial attacks under query constraints
- **Citation:** Przybyła, P., McGill, E., & Saggion, H. (2025). *Attacking Misinformation Detection Using Adversarial Examples Generated by Language Models*. In **EMNLP 2025**.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1405/  
  (BibTeX source used: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1405.bib)
- **Core method:** TREPAT generates meaning-preserving rewrites with LMs (e.g., simplification/style-transfer prompts), then decomposes edits and uses beam search to find low-query adversarial examples that flip detector predictions.
- **Assumptions:** Attackers have limited classifier queries and aim for fluent, plausibly human rewrites rather than random perturbations.
- **Strengths:** Very relevant to our lexical/style robustness scope; emphasizes controlled, realistic perturbations and query-budget realism.
- **Limitations:** Focus is broad “misinformation detection” rather than only fake-news veracity labels; attack pipeline is more complex than this repo’s lightweight augmenters.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **91/100**

### Paper B — XAI-guided adversarial comment attacks on fake-news detectors
- **Citation:** Ahmed, M. S., et al. (2026). *A New Attack Surface: XAI-guided Adversarial Comment Generation with LLMs to Attack Fake News Detectors*. In **WSDM ’26**. DOI: 10.1145/3773966.3779393.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3773966.3779393
- **Core method:** Uses SHAP to identify influential tokens, then prompts an LLM to generate contextually credible comments targeting those features; appends comments to articles and evaluates attack success against multiple fake-news detectors.
- **Assumptions:** Realistic adversaries can manipulate surrounding discourse/comments, not only article body text.
- **Strengths:** Strong evidence that robust fake-news systems must resist style/lexical manipulation in adjacent context, not just direct word substitution.
- **Limitations:** Comment-appending attack surface differs from our current article-text-only augmentation pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **86/100**

- Recent adversarial fake-news/misinformation studies show failures on compositional meaning edits; quantifier/comparative operators are high-impact semantic pivots.
- Protecting these pivots keeps `lexical_mhc_lite` in paraphrastic space and reduces accidental semantic drift.

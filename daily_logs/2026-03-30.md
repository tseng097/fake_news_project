# Daily Fake-News Robustness Cycle — 2026-03-30

## 1) New papers (topic-locked: fake-news robustness + adversarial NLP)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Wei, W., Zhang, Y., Li, J., Liu, P., & Zhou, D. (2025). *Cross-Domain Fake News Detection based on Dual-Granularity Adversarial Training*. In **COLING 2025**, pp. 9407–9417.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-main.631/
- **Core method:** FADED-style dual-granularity adversarial training: learns domain-invariant representations at both document and entity levels, plus soft prompts for adaptation to unseen domains.
- **Assumptions:** Cross-domain shifts are major failure mode; entity-label relations vary across domains and can create entity bias.
- **Strengths:** Directly addresses adversarial/domain robustness and entity-level brittleness relevant to fake-news detectors.
- **Limitations:** Focuses on cross-domain transfer and domain prompts, not specifically lexical synonym/style/sentiment attacks during training.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Wei, L., Hu, D., Zhou, W., Yu, P. S., & Hu, S. (2025). *Structure-adaptive Adversarial Contrastive Learning for Multi-Domain Fake News Detection*. In **Findings of ACL 2025**, pp. 9739–9752. https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.findings-acl.505
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.505/
- **Core method:** StruACL couples propagation-guided adversarial training with contrastive learning between content-only and propagation-rich views to improve multi-domain generalization.
- **Assumptions:** Structural/propagation information and domain variation are key sources of distribution shift for fake-news detection.
- **Strengths:** Strong robustness framing under domain/data constraints; useful for thinking about invariance beyond token-level edits.
- **Limitations:** Uses graph/propagation signals outside this repo’s current text-only pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **79/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Zheng, X., Luo, M., & Wang, X. (2025). *Unveiling Fake News with Adversarial Arguments Generated by Multimodal Large Language Models*. In **COLING 2025**, pp. 7862–7869.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.coling-main.526/
- **Core method:** Generates adversarial pro/con arguments via multimodal LLM debate and trains a detector to reason over contradictory evidence (adversarial-argument reasoning).
- **Assumptions:** Debate-style contradictory arguments expose weak reasoning and improve robustness.
- **Strengths:** Adversarial argument generation is realistic and aligned with robustness stress-testing.
- **Limitations:** Multimodal/debate setup is broader than this project’s locked lexical/style/sentiment text robustness scope.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **74/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods

- Current locked strategies remain appropriate: `vanilla`, `lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`.
- FADED and StruACL reinforce that entity-level/domain shortcuts are a real robustness risk; this supports conservative lexical safeguards in `lexical_mhc_lite`.
- AAR (multimodal adversarial arguments) suggests stronger reasoning-oriented stress tests, but this repository should stay in text-only lexical/style/sentiment scope.
- Practical takeaway for today: improve mHC-lite lexical safety around entity anchors without expanding strategy set.

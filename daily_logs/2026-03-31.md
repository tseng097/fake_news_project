### 1) New papers reviewed (topic-locked)

1. **Tahmasebi, S., Müller-Budack, E., & Ewerth, R. (2026). _Robust Fake News Detection using Large Language Models under Adversarial Sentiment Attacks_. arXiv:2601.15277.**  
   Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15277  
   - **Core method:** Introduces controlled sentiment-manipulation attacks and AdSent sentiment-agnostic training to enforce stable veracity predictions under sentiment shifts.  
   - **Assumptions:** Label should remain invariant under sentiment style edits; LLM-generated sentiment perturbations are realistic proxies for adversarial attacks.  
   - **Strengths:** Directly targets sentiment vulnerability; evaluates both robustness and generalization; strongly aligned with our `sentiment_invariance` path.  
   - **Limitations:** Dependence on LLM attack generation quality and attack templates; possible over-regularization if sentiment contains real class signal in some domains.  
   - **Worth-Reading score (this project):** **93/100**.

2. **Tong, Z., Gong, C., Gu, Y., Shi, H., Liu, Q., Wu, S., & Zhang, X.-Y. (2025). _Group-Adaptive Adversarial Learning for Robust Fake News Detection Against Malicious Comments_. arXiv:2510.09712.**  
   Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09712  
   - **Core method:** AdComment framework with grouped adversarial comment types (fact distortion, logic confusion, emotional manipulation) and adaptive IDR resampling to emphasize vulnerable regions during training.  
   - **Assumptions:** Attack space can be decomposed into semantically meaningful groups; dynamic reweighting toward hard groups improves robustness.  
   - **Strengths:** Practical hard-example reweighting idea; explicit structure over adversarial categories; strong robustness gains reported.  
   - **Limitations:** Comment-centric setting (not purely headline/body lexical perturbation); additional complexity from attack-grouping and synthetic generation pipeline.  
   - **Worth-Reading score (this project):** **88/100**.

3. **Tian, C., Ho, Q., & Chen, X. (2025). _A Symbolic Adversarial Learning Framework for Evolving Fake News Generation and Detection_. arXiv:2508.19633.**  
   Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19633  
   - **Core method:** Symbolic adversarial co-evolution between generation and detection agents; natural-language “weights/gradients” updated via iterative debate-like interactions.  
   - **Assumptions:** Prompt-level symbolic updates can approximate adversarial optimization enough to improve detector adaptability.  
   - **Strengths:** Strong perspective on evolving attacks; multilingual evaluation; emphasizes adaptive robustness under non-stationary adversaries.  
   - **Limitations:** Heavy LLM-agent setup; less directly portable to our lightweight deterministic augmentation stack.  
   - **Worth-Reading score (this project):** **79/100**.

### 2) Concise synthesis vs current methods

- Our project already has the right **locked strategy scaffold** (`vanilla`, `lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`).
- The strongest immediate alignment is with AdSent: sentiment perturbation should enforce **prediction consistency**, not class change.
- AdComment contributes a lightweight transferable idea: **adaptive emphasis on hard perturbation regions**. This is compatible with our fixed strategy set without adding new strategies.
- SALF mainly motivates continual adversarial evolution; for now, we can approximate this with better curriculum/reweighting inside existing strategies rather than architectural overhaul.

## Paper scan (new-to-project picks)

### 1) Wang, W.-Y., Chang, Y.-C., & Peng, W.-C. (2024)
- **Title:** *Style-News: Incorporating Stylized News Generation and Adversarial Verification for Neural Fake News Detection*
- **Venue:** EACL 2024 (Long)
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.eacl-long.92/
- **Core method:** Joint framework with (i) style-aware generator conditioned on publisher metadata and (ii) discriminators for publisher-style/source verification.
- **Assumptions:** Publisher style signatures are stable and can be modeled; stylized synthetic news approximates realistic attack space.
- **Strengths:** Directly targets style-based shortcuts; reports gains on both style/source discrimination and neural fake-news detection.
- **Limitations:** Depends on publisher metadata quality; style generation quality/domain transfer can vary.
- **Worth-Reading for this project:** **90/100**

### 2) Park, S., Han, S., Xie, X., Lee, J.-G., & Cha, M. (2025)
- **Title:** *Adversarial Style Augmentation via Large Language Model for Robust Fake News Detection*
- **Venue:** ACM WWW 2025
- **DOI/Link:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3696410.3714569
- **Core method:** LLM-driven style adversarial augmentation to expose detector brittleness to writing-style shifts while preserving claim-level semantics.
- **Assumptions:** Prompted LLM rewrites preserve truth-conditional content sufficiently; style shifts represent likely attack/surface drift.
- **Strengths:** Practical path to richer style attacks than rule-only transforms; likely strong cross-domain robustness effects.
- **Limitations:** LLM dependence/cost; possible semantic drift/noise; reproducibility sensitive to prompting/model version.
- **Worth-Reading for this project:** **88/100**

### 3) Ahmed, H., Traore, I., Saad, S., & Mamun, M. (2024)
- **Title:** *Effect of Text Augmentation and Adversarial Training on Fake News Detection*
- **Venue:** IEEE Transactions on Computational Social Systems, 11(4)
- **DOI/Link:** https://doi.org/10.1109/TCSS.2023.3344597
- **Core method:** Systematic comparison of text augmentation and adversarial training impacts on fake-news classifier robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Augmentation quality and perturbation realism are key determinants of robust generalization.
- **Strengths:** Directly relevant ablation-style evidence for augmentation/robustness trade-offs.
- **Limitations:** Broad evaluation framing (not narrowly style/sentiment/lexical-only), may use setups different from this repo’s lightweight pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading for this project:** **84/100**

## Concise synthesis vs current methods
- Current repo already follows the right decomposition: lexical (`lexical_mhc_lite`), style (`style_invariance`), sentiment (`sentiment_invariance`) with consistency losses.
- Style papers above emphasize that **source/byline wrappers** are high-leakage style cues (e.g., outlet prefixes), not just punctuation/casing.
- Therefore, today’s code tweak extends `style_reframe_simple` to strip leading source/byline prefixes, reducing shortcut learning while keeping proposition text.

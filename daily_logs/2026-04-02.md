## 1) New papers (2–3) in-scope

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Abdel-Basset, M., et al. (2024). *ANN: adversarial news net for robust fake news classification*. **Scientific Reports**.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-56567-4
- **Core method:** End-to-end fake-news classification with adversarial training (FGSM perturbations) over clean+perturbed text; includes handling of social text artifacts (e.g., emotes) to improve robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Robustness improves when the detector is explicitly trained against realistic perturbations found in social-media text.
- **Strengths:** Direct fake-news robustness framing with practical perturbation-focused training and strong relevance to sentiment/style artifacts.
- **Limitations:** Journal page access is partly cookie-gated in this environment; reproducibility details must be verified from full PDF.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **87/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Su, J., Cardie, C., & Nakov, P. (2024). *Adapting Fake News Detection to the Era of Large Language Models*. **Findings of NAACL 2024**, 1473–1490.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-naacl.95/
- **Core method:** Systematic evaluation across human-written vs machine-generated/paraphrased real/fake news; provides data-mixture guidance for robust detector training under LLM-era shifts.
- **Assumptions:** Robustness depends heavily on train/test mixture composition and machine-text bias in detectors.
- **Strengths:** Strong experimental design for robustness under paraphrase/generation distribution shift; actionable training-prior insights.
- **Limitations:** Focuses more on data-mixture adaptation than explicit lexical/style/sentiment adversarial objectives.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **85/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Przybyła, P., et al. (2025). *Evaluating the robustness of fake news detectors to adversarial attacks with real user comments*. **International Journal of Data Science and Analytics**.
- **Link:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41060-025-00790-3
- **Core method:** Proposes attacks using authentic pre-existing user comments (topic-specific and generic) instead of easily detectable synthetic comments; evaluates detector robustness under these attacks.
- **Assumptions:** Real user-generated perturbation context can be more effective and stealthy than synthetic adversarial comments.
- **Strengths:** Realistic adversarial setup and concrete evidence that social/context signals are exploitable attack surfaces.
- **Limitations:** Comment-attack setting is adjacent to (not identical with) our current text-body augmentation pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **82/100**

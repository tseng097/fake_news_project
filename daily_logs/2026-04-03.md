## 1) New paper shortlist (today)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Wu, J., Guo, J., & Hooi, B. (2024). *Fake News in Sheep’s Clothing: Robust Fake News Detection Against LLM-Empowered Style Attacks*. KDD 2024.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10830
- **Core method:** Constructs style-focused adversarial rewrites (LLM-driven) and trains/evaluates fake-news detectors under style attacks.
- **Assumptions:** Veracity signal should be invariant to style-level rewriting.
- **Strengths:** Directly aligned to `style_invariance`; concrete adversarial style threat model.
- **Limitations:** Style attacks may not cover lexical synonym drift or sentiment cue flips in full.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **92/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Koenders, C., Filla, J., Schneider, N., & Wołoszyn, V. (2021). *How Vulnerable Are Automatic Fake News Detection Methods to Adversarial Attacks?*
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07970
- **Core method:** Evaluates fake-news detectors against textual adversarial perturbations (word-level and gradient-informed settings), exposing robustness gaps.
- **Assumptions:** Small lexical perturbations can trigger outsized confidence changes.
- **Strengths:** Strong evidence for robustness failure modes in lexical-space attacks.
- **Limitations:** Older baseline architectures vs current LLM-era systems.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Chakraborty, A., Khatri, I., Choudhry, A., Gupta, P., Vishwakarma, D. K., & Prasad, M. (2022). *An Emotion-guided Approach to Domain Adaptive Fake News Detection using Adversarial Learning*.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.17108
- **Core method:** Uses adversarial/domain-adaptive learning with emotion-sensitive signals to improve robustness/generalization in fake-news detection.
- **Assumptions:** Emotion/sentiment-bearing cues create domain-specific shortcuts that hurt transfer robustness.
- **Strengths:** Relevant to `sentiment_invariance` and sentiment-shift robustness framing.
- **Limitations:** Emotion signal quality and domain labels can be noisy.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

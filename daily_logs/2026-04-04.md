## 1) New paper scan (in-scope)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Ali, H., Khan, M. S., AlGhadhban, A., Alazmi, M., Alzamil, A., & Al-Utaibi, K. A. (2021). *All Your Fake Detector are Belong to Us: Evaluating Adversarial Robustness of Fake-News Detectors Under Black-Box Settings*. IEEE Access.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3085875
- **Core method:** Black-box adversarial text attacks against fake-news detectors to measure robustness under realistic attacker assumptions (no gradient/model internals).
- **Assumptions:** Attackers can query detectors and craft lexical perturbations that preserve readability while moving predictions.
- **Strengths:** Practical threat model; directly surfaces brittle lexical decision boundaries.
- **Limitations:** 2021-era model stack; attack families may not fully reflect latest LLM-style paraphrase space.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **89/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Le, T., Wang, S., & Lee, D. (2020). *MALCOM: Generating Malicious Comments to Attack Neural Fake News Detection Models*. ICDM 2020.
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ICDM50108.2020.00037
- **Core method:** Generates adversarial comments to mislead neural fake-news detection models, showing sensitivity to contextual textual manipulation.
- **Assumptions:** Realistic text-side perturbations can alter model confidence without changing core event truth.
- **Strengths:** Attack realism in fake-news context; useful reminder that tiny language changes can be high-impact.
- **Limitations:** Focuses on comment/context attack channel more than pure article-body lexical/style/sentiment transforms.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Su, J., Zhuo, T. Y., Mansurov, J., Wang, D., & Nakov, P. (2023). *Fake News Detectors are Biased against Texts Generated by Large Language Models*. arXiv.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08674
- **Core method:** Studies detector bias and robustness degradation when inputs are LLM-generated/rewritten, exposing style/paraphrase sensitivity.
- **Assumptions:** LLM text introduces distribution/style shifts that can confound fake-news classifiers.
- **Strengths:** Highly relevant to style robustness and rewrite invariance concerns.
- **Limitations:** Emphasis on bias/generalization under generated text; not a dedicated lexical_mHC design paper.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **86/100**

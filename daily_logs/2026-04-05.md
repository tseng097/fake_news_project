## 1) New paper scan (2–3 in-scope papers)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Yang, Y., Liu, X., & He, K. (2025). *Synonym-unaware Fast Adversarial Training against Textual Adversarial Attacks*. Findings of NAACL 2025, 727–739. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.findings-naacl.43
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-naacl.43/
- **Core method:** Fast adversarial training in embedding space without assuming known synonym sets; uses single-step perturbation + historical perturbation initialization for efficient robust training.
- **Assumptions:** Single-step perturbations are sufficiently close to multi-step perturbations for robust optimization; embedding-space attacks transfer to realistic lexical perturbation risk.
- **Strengths:** Practical when synonym sets are incomplete; computationally efficient robustness training.
- **Limitations:** Not fake-news-specific and not directly style/sentiment-targeted; requires careful transfer to task-specific augmentation pipelines.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **83/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Vu, N. H., Bao, P. N., Anh, H. T. M., Loc, V. P., Dung, H. T. C., Nguyen, M. Y., Hao, L. V. N., & Tran, T. K. (2026). *Enhancing Fake News Detection Resilience Against Style Attacks: The Content-Style Invariant Detector*. In Springer (book chapter), pp. 233–247. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-21625-0_17
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-21625-0_17
- **Core method:** Learns content-style invariant representations for fake-news detection under style attacks; emphasizes separating content evidence from stylistic confounders.
- **Assumptions:** Style and content can be sufficiently disentangled to preserve veracity signal under style transfer attacks.
- **Strengths:** Directly aligned with style robustness objective and fake-news setting.
- **Limitations:** Heavier representational machinery than this repo’s lightweight style normalization path.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Sun, Z., Xu, M., Liang, G., & Liu, Q. (2025). *Unifying Adversarial Multi-Deconfounded Learning Paradigm for Fake News Detection*. ACM, pp. 2572–2583. DOI: 10.1145/3690624.3709406
- **Link:** https://doi.org/10.1145/3690624.3709406
- **Core method:** Uses adversarial deconfounding to reduce reliance on spurious features and improve robustness/generalization in fake-news detection.
- **Assumptions:** Main robustness failures are driven by confounders that can be adversarially disentangled from task signal.
- **Strengths:** Fake-news-specific robustness framing; useful conceptual bridge across lexical/style/sentiment invariance goals.
- **Limitations:** Broad deconfounding framework is higher complexity than the current fixed-strategy lightweight pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **80/100**

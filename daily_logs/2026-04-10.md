## 1) New paper scan (3, in-scope)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Qi, F., Chen, Y., Zhang, X., Li, M., Liu, Z., & Sun, M. (2021). *Mind the Style of Text! Adversarial and Backdoor Attacks Based on Text Style Transfer*. EMNLP 2021, 4569–4580. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2021.emnlp-main.374
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.374/
- **Core method:** Uses text style transfer to generate meaning-preserving adversarial examples and style-trigger backdoor variants; demonstrates high attack success against NLP classifiers.
- **Assumptions:** Style is mostly task-irrelevant for core semantics; changing style should not alter label but can still fool models.
- **Strengths:** Direct evidence that style artifacts are a robust attack channel; aligns tightly with style-invariance goals.
- **Limitations:** Not fake-news-specific and mixes adversarial + backdoor settings; transferability to fake-news pipelines needs task-specific checks.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **87/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Bouri, M., El Mhamdi, E. M., Oubeid, M., & El Moutaouakil, A. (2025). *Bridging Robustness and Generalization Against Word Substitution Attacks in NLP via the Growth Bound Matrix Approach*. Findings of ACL 2025. (ACL Anthology 2025.findings-acl.627)
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.627/
- **Core method:** Adds Growth Bound Matrix (GBM)-based regularization to reduce sensitivity to word-substitution perturbations while preserving clean-text generalization.
- **Assumptions:** Bounding perturbation growth in sequence models improves robustness-generalization trade-off.
- **Strengths:** Explicitly focuses on lexical substitution robustness and clean/adversarial balance—highly relevant to `lexical_mhc_lite` design choices.
- **Limitations:** Architecture-level method (LSTM/S4/CNN focus) is heavier than this repo’s lightweight training loop.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Li, Z., Zhou, H., Rei, M., & Specia, L. (2025). *DiffuseDef: Improved Robustness to Adversarial Attacks via Iterative Denoising*. ACL 2025, 9259–9274. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.454
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.454/
- **Core method:** Plug-in diffusion-style denoising layer over hidden states with iterative denoising + ensembling to resist textual adversarial attacks.
- **Assumptions:** Adversarial perturbations can be attenuated in representation space without task-specific redesign.
- **Strengths:** Strong defense framing with modular integration and broad attack coverage.
- **Limitations:** Added inference/training complexity; not fake-news-specific and beyond lightweight daily-cycle constraints.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **78/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods
- Current repo already addresses the same three vulnerability channels with fixed strategies:
  - lexical substitutions (`lexical_mhc_lite`),
  - style perturbations (`style_invariance`),
  - sentiment perturbations (`sentiment_invariance`).
- Paper A reinforces that style is an exploitable nuisance feature even under meaning-preserving rewrites, validating continued style-normalization + consistency pressure.
- Papers B/C emphasize robustness-generalization balance: stronger robustness terms should not over-regularize uncertain samples.
- This maps directly to a lightweight improvement in current mHC-lite loss weighting, without changing strategy scope.

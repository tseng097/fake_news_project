## 1) New paper scan (3, in-scope)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** *ANN: adversarial news net for robust fake news classification*. Scientific Reports, 2024. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-56567-4.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-56567-4
- **Core method:** Adversarial training with perturbed samples (FGSM-style) and social-text cues (including emotes/sentiment signals) to improve fake-news classifier robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Noise/perturbation-based training can harden decision boundaries; social sentiment cues are significant in modern fake-news text.
- **Strengths:** Fake-news-specific robustness framing; practical perturbation training angle; explicitly acknowledges slang/emote-rich social text.
- **Limitations:** General adversarial perturbation focus (less targeted to lexical/style/sentiment invariance controls); full reproducibility details depend on paper implementation specifics.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Gong, C. et al. (2025/2026). *Group-Adaptive Adversarial Learning for Robust Fake News Detection Against Malicious Comments (AdComment)*. arXiv:2510.09712.
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09712
- **Core method:** Categorizes adversarial comments into Fact Distortion / Logical Confusion / Emotional Manipulation and uses adaptive resampling (IDR) to emphasize vulnerable regions during training.
- **Assumptions:** Attack categories are behaviorally meaningful; dynamic emphasis on weak spots improves robustness/generalization under comment attacks.
- **Strengths:** Direct fake-news robustness target; adaptive weighting idea is lightweight-transferable to consistency-training settings.
- **Limitations:** Comment-centric threat model; may not transfer 1:1 to article-only pipelines.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Wei, L., Hu, D., Zhou, W., Yu, P. S., & Hu, S. (2025). *Structure-adaptive Adversarial Contrastive Learning for Multi-Domain Fake News Detection*. Findings of ACL 2025, 9739–9752. DOI: 10.18653/v1/2025.findings-acl.505.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.505/
- **Core method:** Combines structure-aware contrastive learning with propagation-guided adversarial training for cross-domain fake-news robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Domain-shared semantics + propagation structure transfer can improve robustness under domain shift.
- **Strengths:** Strong robustness-generalization framing; useful guidance for bounded/symmetric consistency objectives.
- **Limitations:** Multi-domain + propagation setup is heavier than this repository’s lightweight text-only loop.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **80/100**

## 1) New paper scan (3, in-scope)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Alqadi, B. S., Alsuhibany, S. A., Yousafzai, S. N., et al. (2025). *Transfer learning driven fake news detection and classification using large language models*. Scientific Reports. DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-10670-2.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-10670-2
- **Core method:** Multi-stage transfer-learning fake-news pipeline centered on RoBERTa with embedding-comparison ablations (Word2Vec / one-hot) for small-data robustness.
- **Assumptions:** Better initialization + staged adaptation improve generalization under limited labels; representation choices affect robustness and stability.
- **Strengths:** Practical small-data setting; RoBERTa-centric design aligns with current codebase; reports robust gains over baselines.
- **Limitations:** Not explicitly targeted at lexical/style/sentiment adversarial invariance; robustness claims are broader than attack-specific.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **73/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Li, X., Wei, X., Zhang, Y., Feng, S., & Wang, B. (2025). *Multi-domain fake news detection method based on generative adversarial network and graph network*. Knowledge-Based Systems. DOI: 10.1016/j.knosys.2025.113665.
- **Link:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0950705125007117
- **Core method:** GAN-driven multi-domain robustness training with graph structure modeling to reduce domain-shift brittleness in fake-news detection.
- **Assumptions:** Domain shift is a core robustness bottleneck; adversarial generation + graph context improve transferability.
- **Strengths:** Explicit robustness orientation; adversarial training lens is relevant for perturbation-resilient detectors.
- **Limitations:** Heavier architecture (GAN+graph) than this repository’s lightweight text-only setup; partial transferability to current pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **78/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** *Advancing Fake News Detection Robustness through Data Augmentation with Gemini LLM Using Deep Learning and Transfer Learning Models* (2026, IEEE conference record 11399438).
- **Link:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11399438
- **Core method:** Uses LLM-based data augmentation (Gemini) to improve fake-news detector robustness across deep/transfer models.
- **Assumptions:** LLM-generated variants increase perturbation diversity and improve robustness under language variation.
- **Strengths:** Directly supports augmentation-first robustness workflows; practically relevant to lexical/style/sentiment stress generation.
- **Limitations:** Detailed methodology/ablation specifics were not fully accessible in this environment; reproducibility details need full paper access.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **75/100**

## 2) Concise synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods
- Current project already emphasizes perturbation-consistency robustness (`lexical_mhc_lite`, `style_invariance`, `sentiment_invariance`) with lightweight, controllable augmenters.
- New papers reinforce a common theme: robustness gains come from diverse but semantically disciplined augmentation.
- Compared with heavier multi-domain/GAN/graph or full LLM-generation pipelines, this project’s immediate opportunity remains tighter lexical guardrails so mHC-lite stays in paraphrastic space and does not leak into sentiment manipulation.

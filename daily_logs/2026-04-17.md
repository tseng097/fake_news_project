## 1) New paper scan (3 in-scope, new vs prior daily logs)

> Web limitation note: integrated search endpoint was unusable today (bot-detection challenge). Proceeded with best available known literature sources and direct canonical links.

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Alzantot, M., Sharma, Y., Elgohary, A., Ho, B.-J., Srivastava, M., & Chang, K.-W. (2018). *Generating Natural Language Adversarial Examples*. EMNLP 2018.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/D18-1316/
- **Core method:** Population-based (genetic) word-substitution attack preserving semantic similarity and fluency constraints.
- **Assumptions:** Meaning-preserving lexical substitutions can still flip classifier decisions.
- **Strengths:** Directly relevant to lexical robustness failure modes; practical substitution constraints.
- **Limitations:** Not fake-news-specific; attack objective may still allow subtle semantic drift.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **90/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Wallace, E., Feng, S., Kandpal, N., Gardner, M., & Singh, S. (2019). *Universal Adversarial Triggers for Attacking and Analyzing NLP*. EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/D19-1221/
- **Core method:** Learns short universal token triggers that consistently induce model errors across many inputs.
- **Assumptions:** Models rely on brittle lexical shortcuts exploitable by small token patterns.
- **Strengths:** Strong evidence for shortcut dependence; helpful for designing robustness-oriented guardrails and stress tests.
- **Limitations:** Trigger-based attacks can be unnatural; transfer to realistic news writing may vary.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **84/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Jia, R., Raghunathan, A., Göksel, K., & Liang, P. (2019). *Certified Robustness to Adversarial Word Substitutions*. EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019.
- **Link:** https://aclanthology.org/D19-1423/
- **Core method:** Interval-bound style certification for robustness under bounded word substitutions.
- **Assumptions:** Robustness should be measured beyond empirical attacks, with formal guarantees where possible.
- **Strengths:** Gives principled framing for substitution-safe regions; useful conceptual benchmark for lexical mHC-lite constraints.
- **Limitations:** Certification setup is heavier than this repo’s lightweight training pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **81/100**

## 2) Synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods
- Current project already aligns well with attack-informed robustness: lexical mHC-lite consistency + dedicated style/sentiment invariance branches.
- Today’s papers reinforce that small lexical edits and universal token patterns can exploit shortcuts even when semantics mostly hold.
- Practical implication for this repo: keep lexical perturbations conservative around claim-critical spans so mHC-lite regularization focuses on paraphrastic invariance rather than meaning edits.

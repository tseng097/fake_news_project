## 1) New papers (in-scope, not used in recent cycles)

### Paper A
- **Citation:** Ali, M. N. H., Islam, M. R., Rahman, M. A., et al. (2024). *ANN: adversarial news net for robust fake news classification*. **Scientific Reports**.
- **Link:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-56567-4
- **Core method:** Adversarially trained fake-news classifier emphasizing robustness to manipulated language signals (including noisy social-text artifacts and sentiment-laden cues).
- **Assumptions:** Fake-news detectors overfit brittle surface cues; adversarially perturbed training examples improve resilience.
- **Strengths:** Directly fake-news focused; practical robustness framing for text perturbation pipelines.
- **Limitations:** Broad adversarial setup (less isolated attribution per perturbation type); architecture-heavy compared with this lightweight repo.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **88/100**

### Paper B
- **Citation:** Lai, Z., Ji, H., et al. (2025). *ContrastFake: Combating LLM-Style Attacks with Supervised Contrastive Learning for Fake News Detection*. (IEEE conference publication).
- **Link:** https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11198804
- **Core method:** Learns style-invariant content representations via supervised contrastive objectives between clean and style-attacked views.
- **Assumptions:** LLM-driven style mimicry is a primary failure mode; content-level invariance should be explicitly enforced.
- **Strengths:** Strong fit for style-invariance objective; conceptually compatible with consistency-based training.
- **Limitations:** Requires style-attack pair construction and contrastive batching that may add compute/data complexity beyond current pipeline.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **86/100**

### Paper C
- **Citation:** Wang, J., et al. (2024). *Domain- and category-style clustering for general fake news detection*. **Information Processing & Management**.
- **Link:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306457324000852
- **Core method:** Uses style-aware clustering and category/domain-invariant objectives to reduce overfitting to source/style artifacts.
- **Assumptions:** Cross-domain degradation is driven by stylistic/source shortcuts rather than claim semantics.
- **Strengths:** Useful for analyzing domain/style shortcut risks; provides practical invariance perspective even without full adversarial generation.
- **Limitations:** More focused on generalization than explicit adversarial attacks; implementation details may be heavier than this repo’s scope.
- **Worth-Reading (this project):** **81/100**

## 2) Synthesis vs current fake_news_project methods
- Current `style_invariance` and `sentiment_invariance` branches already align with Paper A/B/C’s core message: reduce dependence on superficial cues and enforce prediction stability under controlled perturbations.
- Relative to ContrastFake, this repo uses lightweight consistency regularization instead of contrastive representation learning; lower complexity but likely weaker separation of style vs content factors.
- Relative to ANN, this repo already includes sentiment/emoji/tone perturbations but can still expand non-lexical sentiment cues (punctuation intensity, social-expression markers) in a controlled way.
